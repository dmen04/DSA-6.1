**User:** i still dont get it how it differentiates from chi and ttest

**Claude:** Explained the difference clearly:
- **Chi-squared test** — used when comparing two categorical variables (e.g. gender vs pet preference). Used in class exercises.
- **T-test** — used when comparing a numerical variable between two groups (e.g. is alcohol level different between red and white wine?)
- **Correlation** — not a hypothesis test. Measures how strongly two numerical variables are related (e.g. does higher alcohol mean higher quality?)

In this dataset: color is the categorical grouping variable (red/white), and alcohol/acidity/pH etc are the numerical variables being compared → t-test.

---

**User:** so correlation is not under hypothesis like ttest or chi?

**Claude:** Confirmed. Correlation is under Data Exploration, not Hypothesis Testing. It just measures relationship strength, it does not prove or disprove anything. T-tests are under Hypothesis Testing.
